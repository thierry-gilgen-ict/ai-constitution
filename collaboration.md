# Collaboration

Load for authorized coordination, independent review or continuation.

- One accountable coordinator owns assignment, integration and acceptance. One bounded issue or owner assignment identifies the outcome and authoritative tracker.
- Default limits: two active workers and two unfinished implementation candidates per coordinator. Count review backlog; examine aged candidates weekly before starting unrelated work.
- Delegate only when the user or applicable instructions authorize it. Reserve exclusive paths; avoid recursive delegation and competing implementations without a comparison assignment.
- Preserve dirty worktrees, ignored receipts and source custody; use clean isolated branches. Never reset, stash, rebase, overwrite or retire another worker's work.
- Reviewers use independent context and examine exact source and negative cases, never their own implementation. Re-review changed source; keep human/platform approval separate.
- Resume in the existing issue/PR record: task ID, owner, repo/branch/SHA, state, next authorized action, blocker, evidence and resource checkpoint. History belongs in immutable evidence, not active routing policy.
- At most two coherent initial attempts plus one authorized bounded escalation, subject to stricter or consumed limits. Missing capability/authority blocks dependent work; a new branch/model/session never resets counters.
- Check retained PR-base dependencies and custody before separately authorized worktree retirement. Never automatically delete historical or dirty work.
