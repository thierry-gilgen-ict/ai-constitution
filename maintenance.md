# Maintenance

Load for instruction, route and managed-installation upkeep.

- Reuse build/check/scan, doctor/explain, managed onboarding, pins, private journals and guarded rollback. Preview exact targets; sync --all is not a single-project transaction.
- Core and project contract each stay within 8 KiB; specialist modules within 6 KiB. Flag active continuation/state above 64 KiB. Explicit archives and immutable PRs are historical, not always-loaded policy.
- Reconcile conflicting active authority and duplicated model tables with their owner. Preserve uncommitted evidence before approved archival; do not create another giant active ledger.
- Check version/pin, known conflicts, branch/check drift and aged candidates weekly or on relevant PRs with existing CLI/CI. No paid polling, new benchmarks/platform or scheduling without authorization.
- Separate generated, installed, fresh-session loaded, model-access and accepted-outcome evidence. Definitions/hashes do not prove account access or publisher authenticity.
- Measure accepted outcomes, billing-provenance cost, integration lead time, first-pass acceptance, review/rework and escaped defects over an agreed 2–4-week window. Routes stay provisional until matched evidence supports promotion.
