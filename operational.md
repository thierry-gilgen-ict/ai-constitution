# Operational work

Load for release, deployment, publication, paid execution or recovery.

- Prepare a reviewable exact-revision candidate and relevant CI before human release/deployment approval. Integration and infrastructure health do not establish product acceptance.
- Require current target-specific authority, finite scope/budget, fail-closed publication and appropriate backup/restore evidence. Historical allowances and renamed tasks do not renew exhausted retries.
- Stop repeated batches at systemic capability, entitlement, persistence or semantic acceptance failures. Job termination, schema and complete domain fields are distinct checks.
- Verify the exact artifact and behavior in the stated environment. Record owner semantic/business acceptance; never substitute seed/sample data or stale publication for fresh results.
- Keep a private task/PR/revision receipt with attempts, review/rework, lead time, requested/actual route, resource closure and billing provenance or UNKNOWN. Separate cached/uncached tokens, charges, subscriptions and task attribution.
- Preserve platform-managed Bots' operator roles, read-only access, room isolation, approved outbound/publishing authority and asynchronous handoffs. Native coding clients retain their own scoped permissions.
