# Research

Use this module when the task depends on external evidence or changing facts.

- Resolve what decision the research supports and what would change that decision.
- Prefer primary sources: official documentation, original research, public records, and first-party release notes.
- Verify current model identifiers, availability, pricing, APIs, and deprecations. Do not infer a product's availability from a provider's API catalog.
- Open the underlying source; search snippets are leads. Record the source URL and verification date for facts stored in the registry.
- Separate provider claims from local measurements. A benchmark result is evidence for the workload tested, not a universal ranking.
- Identify disagreement and missing evidence. Keep uncertainty visible rather than inventing precision.
- Cite sources close to the claims they support. Use short quotations only when wording itself matters.
- Minimize the data needed for a query. Do not send private project material to a new provider without authorization.

- Verify semantic coverage separately from job health and schema: required financial snapshots, source lineage and freshness cannot be inferred from a completed batch.
- Bound paid experiments before dispatch and stop at systemic blockers. Keep accepted-outcome cost evidence private; counters are not billable charges.
